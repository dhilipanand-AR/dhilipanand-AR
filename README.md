## 🔬 Academic Project

### *Preventative and Therapeutic Potential of Coumarin Compounds from Hemidesmus indicus Against Peptic Ulcer Disease*

My academic project explored the **preventative and therapeutic potential of coumarin compounds from* Hemidesmus indicus*** in relation to peptic ulcer disease.

The work involved reviewing scientific research related to **phytochemical screening, antioxidant activity, anti-inflammatory effects, gastroprotective potential, anti-ulcer studies and computational approaches**.

📄 **Research:** Project work has also been developed into a published paper.
